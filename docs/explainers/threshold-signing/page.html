Two separate derivation roles

Provisioning protocolStreaming Yao

Separate secrets stay separate

The press combines signing contributions. It never represents a complete private key assembled at Router. Sealed cartridges remain in their own holders.

Two different origins

The client’s wallet custody seed derives its owner signing roots. The server’s tenant derivation root has A/B role shares. Lane material comes from the curve-specific provisioning protocol.

The trust assumption

One trusted organization operates both Derivers. Role separation assumes their secrets remain separate. An authority that controls both roles falls outside that guarantee.

Mechanical analogy · no cryptography, wallet calls, or real secret material. Motion shows protocol stages. The ECDSA tray shows three example pool entries. Each entry has separately held client and server material.
