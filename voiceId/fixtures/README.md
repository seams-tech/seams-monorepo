# Private VoiceID recording fixtures

Raw voice clips are biometric data. This directory excludes recordings and their
manifests from Git. Keep each manifest with its recordings and preserve the
associated consent and provenance records.

Do not commit, upload, or log this media. Sharing requires every recorded person's
explicit agreement for that use. Keep recordings local or in a separately approved,
encrypted research store.

Source cleanup preserves these recordings. Delete captures only through an explicit
retention decision.
